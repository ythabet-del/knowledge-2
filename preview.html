Suggested scoring

Correct answer: 100 points. Using a hint: −25 points. Optional bonus: up to 20 points for a clear explanation supported by evidence. The facilitator awards points after reviewing the answer.

Suggested team rules

Allow 60–90 seconds to discuss. Rotate teams after each mission. Invite the team to explain its reasoning. If using a bonus, apply the same standard to every team.
